I’ve never been particularly interested in doing things simply because that’s how they’ve always been done.

My career has taken me across some rather different worlds, from health and social care to technology, entrepreneurship and innovation.

What connects them is a fascination with problems that don’t have straightforward answers. Sometimes that leads to an idea. Sometimes to an entirely new business.

Sunstone Systems. Entrepreneurial history

Building technology for the places others couldn’t easily reach.

I co-founded Sunstone in 2011 to put surveillance and communications where there was no power and no network. Over thirteen years it became a family of solar-powered systems for remote industrial sites.

Fyrfly Systems. Trading now

Rethinking physical security.

Fyrfly is a physical security specialist. It works with schools, the public sector and commercial premises as a single accountable partner, from design through to maintenance.

It also publishes free tools for the people who run those buildings, including a self-assessment for Martyn’s Law.

Assistiv. In development

Exploring the missing middle.

Assistiv asks how older adults can be better supported when they fall between living independently and qualifying for formal care, the space existing health and social-care provision tends to miss.

It is early-stage work, and I describe it that way on purpose.
