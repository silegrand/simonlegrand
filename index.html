How I think

I’ve never been particularly interested in doing things simply because that’s how they’ve always been done.

My career has taken me across some rather different worlds, from health and social care to technology, entrepreneurship and innovation.

What connects them is a fascination with problems that don’t have straightforward answers. I want to know why things work the way they do, where conventional thinking falls short and what might happen if we approached them differently.

Sometimes that leads to an idea. Sometimes to an entirely new business. Occasionally it leads somewhere unexpected.

Sunstone Systems. Co-founder, 2011 to 2024

Building technology for the places others couldn’t easily reach.

I co-founded Sunstone in 2011 to put surveillance and communications where there was no power and no network. Over thirteen years that became a family of solar-powered systems for remote industrial sites.

The work took me to Tengiz in Kazakhstan, where 58 systems were deployed with Chevron and Tengizchevroil. It also led to work with Rivian Automotive and Network Rail, and to three granted patents.

One point for each system. No grid connection to any of them. A schematic, not a map.

Fyrfly SystemsFounderCurrent

Rethinking physical security.

Fyrfly is a physical security specialist based in Whitstable. It works with schools, the public sector and commercial premises as a single accountable partner, from design through to maintenance.

It also publishes free tools for the people who run those buildings, including a self-assessment for Martyn’s Law.

Exploring the missing middle.

Assistiv is an initiative in development. It asks how older adults can be better supported when they fall between living independently and qualifying for formal care, the space existing health and social-care provision tends to miss.

It is early-stage work, and I describe it that way on purpose.

Visit assistiv.co
